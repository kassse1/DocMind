Aurora Conference Centre: booking policy

Bookings must be confirmed with a deposit of 25% of the rental fee at least 14 days before the event.

The main hall holds up to 180 seated guests. Each of the two meeting rooms holds 20 guests.

Cancellations made more than 30 days before the event are refunded in full. Cancellations made later are refunded at 50%.

Catering is provided only by the centre's partner, Lumen Kitchen. Outside food is not allowed in any room.

Parking is free for up to 40 cars on weekdays. On weekends parking costs 5 euros per car.
